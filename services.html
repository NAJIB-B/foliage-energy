02 / GOOD TO KNOW

COMMON
QUESTIONS.

Still deciding what is right for your site? Start here, then talk with our team about the details.

What kinds of solar systems do you design?

We work across home and business solar, hybrid systems with battery storage, solar water pumping, community energy concepts and standalone solar lighting. The right option depends on your energy needs and site.

How do you decide what size system I need?

We start with your electricity use, priorities, available space and budget. A site review helps us understand the loads to support and recommend a system that fits the way you use power.

Can a solar system work with the grid or a generator?

Yes. A suitable hybrid design can coordinate solar, batteries and existing power sources. We assess your setup first so the recommended configuration fits your equipment and how you need to use it.

How long does installation take?

Timing depends on the system size, site conditions and any electrical or structural work required. After reviewing your project, we can outline the expected stages and schedule.

Do you provide maintenance and support?

We can discuss system checks and ongoing support for your installation. The right maintenance plan depends on the equipment, site and how the system is used.

How can I get a recommendation or estimate?

Send us a message with your location, the type of site and the loads you want to support. Our team can follow up to learn more and discuss the next step.
